# nietzscheson — design system

The foundation brand for **Cristian Angulo Nova**: a staff software engineer who started in philosophy.

## The idea

Nietzsche wrote in **numbered aphorisms** and thought on walks around the lakes of the Engadin, at Sils-Maria.
The brand borrows both: a cool alpine palette (lake, stone, snow) and the **§** as its signature mark.
The "How I work" principles are set as citable aphorisms (`/#§3`), because the numbering is a real citation convention, not decoration.

## Structure (Atomic Design)

| Level | What lives here |
| --- | --- |
| **Foundations** | Color, type, spacing, motion tokens (`src/styles/global.css`) |
| **Atoms** | Button, TextLink, Heading, Text, Avatar, Icon, Tag, SectionMark, Wordmark, Divider |
| **Molecules** | NavLink, SocialLinks, ThemeToggle, SectionHeader, ServiceItem, Aphorism, RoleItem, PostItem, ProjectItem, CompanyList |
| **Organisms** | SiteHeader (with scroll-spy), Hero, WorkedWith, AboutSection, ServicesSection, PrinciplesSection, ProjectsSection, ExperienceSection (timeline), WritingSection, ContactCTA, SiteFooter |
| **Templates** | SiteLayout, ArticleTemplate |
| **Pages** | Home (the whole site, one page) and Article |

## Rules

- **Spend boldness in one place.** The § aphorisms are the memorable element; everything else stays quiet.
- **Structure is information.** Borders separate items in lists; there are no decorative cards or shadows.
- **One page.** Everything lives on the home page; the nav scrolls to sections and highlights the one on screen. Articles keep their own URLs so they can be shared and indexed.
- **Bands break the rhythm.** About and Contact sit on a raised surface so a long page has landmarks.
- **One motion moment.** The hero headline settles line by line. Nothing else animates on its own. Reduced motion is respected.
- **Sentence case everywhere.** No all-caps eyebrows, no monospace labels, no arrows appended to links (external links get ↗ because they leave the site).
- **Semantic tokens only.** Components use `bg`, `ink`, `body`, `muted`, `line`, `accent`; dark mode swaps the tokens, never the components.

Use the theme switcher in the toolbar to check every story in light and dark.
